## Why

A player should be able to read a committed attack, choose a defense, and land
the intended counter even when several soldiers are close together.

## What Changes

- Give soldiers a fast slash and a slower overhead strike with fixed timing.
- Make aim determine sword contact and retain the staggered target for a counter.
- Show and sound the difference between a miss, an obstruction, a shield hit,
  and a clean hit. Warn the player about incoming attacks and approaching flankers.
- Compare repeatable timing, holding guard, and continuous attacking in game tests.

## Capabilities

The change updates battle play and adds optional combat audio roles to content packs.
