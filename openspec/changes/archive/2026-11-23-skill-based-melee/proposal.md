# Skill-based melee and solid soldiers

## Why

The original battle lets units pass through one another, gives opponents a static
sword pose, and offers the same upgrade for hanging back as for fighting well.

## What Changes

- Give living soldiers solid bodies and let them slide around nearby soldiers and ruins.
- Animate sword wind-ups, contact, and recovery for the player and nearby soldiers.
- Let a timed block open a counterstrike. Charge stamina for each strike or ordinary block.
- Send enemy detachments around the banks to put pressure on the rear.
- Give surviving players a bonus rank for two kills or successful counters.

## Impact

- The affected capabilities are battle-play and campaign-content.
- Existing saves and legacy meshes load in the updated game.
