# Fodder art and sound

The Broken Ford models, soldier sprites, banner texture, pixel font, and original
synthesized game sounds are by the Fodder contributors. The original work and
asset generation source are in the Fodder repository. These assets are licensed
under CC BY 4.0. [Source](https://github.com/stbange/fodder) · [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The flank warning was synthesized with the CMU Flite `kal16` voice and filtered
with FFmpeg. [CMU Flite](https://www.festvox.org/flite/)
